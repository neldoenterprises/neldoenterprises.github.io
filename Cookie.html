Cookie Policy

This page explains how NELDO ENTERPRISES may use cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

Last reviewed: 12 October 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website may store in your browser or on your device. Similar technologies, such as pixels or local storage, can remember preferences or help a website understand how it is used. Our website may use browser storage to remember choices such as your display theme.

2. How these technologies may be used

Depending on the features available on the website, cookies or similar technologies may be used to:

Essential functions

Support basic navigation and features required for a page to work.

Preferences

Remember choices such as light or dark appearance where implemented.

Usage and performance

If analytics are enabled, help us understand general site usage and identify errors.

Third-party features

Support embedded or externally hosted services, which may have their own policies.

3. Types of cookies

Strictly necessary

Used for functions that are needed to provide a requested website feature.

Preference cookies

Remember settings, such as language or display preferences, where supported.

Analytics cookies

May collect aggregated information about how visitors use a site, if an analytics service is configured.

Third-party cookies

May be set by external services embedded in or linked from the website. Their use is governed by the relevant provider's policy.

4. Your choices

You can review, block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you manage cookies by site. If you block certain storage technologies, some website preferences or features may not work as expected.

  • •Use your browser's privacy or site settings to clear existing cookies.
  • •Adjust browser settings to block some or all cookies.
  • •Review the privacy and cookie notices of third-party services you choose to use.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or applicable requirements change. The revised date at the top of this page indicates when it was last reviewed. Please check this page periodically for updates.

6. Privacy and contact

For more information about how personal information may be handled, read our Privacy Policy. If you have questions about this Cookie Policy, contact NELDO ENTERPRISES.
