Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit the NELDO ENTERPRISES website, contact us, or use any features we make available.

Last reviewed: 12 October 2026

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

NELDO ENTERPRISES provides electronics, accessories, fashion products and ICT services. This policy covers information associated with your use of our website and information you choose to share when contacting us about products, orders, repairs or support. Individual services or third-party platforms may have their own privacy terms.

2. Information you may provide

Depending on how you interact with us, you may choose to provide information such as:

Contact details

Your name, phone number, email address and general location when you include them in a message or enquiry.

Orders and service enquiries

Product requests, repair descriptions, order details and other information needed to respond to you.

Messages you send

The content of enquiries or feedback shared through email, phone, WhatsApp or other channels.

Browser preferences

Certain preferences may be stored in your browser, such as your chosen light or dark theme.

3. Information collected automatically

Web hosting providers, browsers or third-party services used by a page may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device details, referring page or access time. We do not claim that every type of analytics or tracking is active on this website. The information actually collected depends on the website configuration and the services enabled.

4. How information may be used

Information may be used where reasonably necessary to:

  • •Respond to questions, product enquiries and requests for technical support.
  • •Coordinate orders, repairs, delivery or other services you request.
  • •Maintain the website and remember preferences where those features are implemented.
  • •Protect the website, investigate misuse and meet applicable legal obligations.
  • •Send updates or promotions where appropriate and permitted by law.

5. When information may be shared

We do not sell personal information to data brokers. Information may be shared when necessary with service providers that help operate the website or deliver a requested service, when you ask us to share it, when disclosure is required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect rights and security. Third-party services, including messaging, hosting or social platforms, handle information under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Cookies and browser storage

The website may use cookies or browser storage to support features such as saving a display preference. Browser storage is not the same as a traditional cookie. You can manage cookies and site data through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some features. Read our Cookie Policy for more information.

7. How long information is kept

We aim to keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose it was provided, to manage an ongoing enquiry or service, to maintain appropriate records, or to meet legal requirements. Actual retention periods depend on the type of information and the circumstances. Information stored on your own device or by third-party platforms may be subject to their separate retention controls.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the website and services in use to protect information. No website, device or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive information unless it is necessary for your request and use trusted channels when contacting us.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may be able to ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal information about you, or object to certain processing. You may also choose not to provide optional information, although we may then be unable to fulfil a related request. We will consider requests in line with applicable legal requirements and may need to verify your identity before responding.

10. Third-party websites and services

Our website may link to other websites or services, such as WhatsApp or external social platforms. We do not control the privacy practices of those providers. Review their privacy information before sharing personal details with them.

11. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services or legal obligations change. The latest review date will be shown at the top of this page. Please review it occasionally for updates.

12. Contact us about privacy

For questions or requests relating to this policy, contact NELDO ENTERPRISES using the options below. Please describe your request clearly and do not include passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.
